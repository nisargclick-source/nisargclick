I'm Nisarg, a London based event and wedding photographer. My work is shaped by a decade of quiet obsession with available light, unguarded moments, and the kind of stillness a shutter can hold.

I specialise in weddings and events but I also shoot portraits, birthdays, and baby showers for clients who want that same candid, story first style for smaller occasions.
